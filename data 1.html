About JJ Institute

Education is not just about scoring marks; it is about building confidence, communication, and lifelong skills. JJ Institute has been committed to nurturing students through structured language education and academic support since 2009, helping the non-Hindi-speaking community of Tamil Nadu gain high-level linguistic proficiency.

What began as a specialized learning centre in Coimbatore has grown into a trusted multi-branch institute offering certified language programmes, academic tuition, and spoken coaching across physical and live online formats.

Academic Programmes & Curriculum

1. Sabha Hindi Examinations (Dakshina Bharat Hindi Prachar Sabha)

A structured, government-recognized progression covering all levels of Hindi proficiency—from foundational script to postgraduate literature. Duration is approximately 6 months per level.

Stage Examination Level Core Curriculum Focus
1 Aksharabhyas / Bala Bodhini Script, alphabet, and basic word formation
2 Parichaya (परिचय) Foundational reading, writing, and elementary grammar
3 Prathamic (प्राथमिक) Elementary grammar, translation, and comprehension
4 Madhyama (मध्यमा) Intermediate grammar, prose, and composition
5 Rashtrabhasha (राष्ट्रभाषा) Advanced grammar and literature study
6 Praveshika (प्रवेशिका) Pre-degree level literary analysis
7 Visharad (Poorvardh & Uttarardh) Degree-equivalent literature and linguistics
8 Praveen (Poorvardh & Uttarardh) Postgraduate-level advanced Hindi studies

2. French Language Programme

Aligned with international CEFR (DELF/DALF) standards for all age groups.

  • Beginner (A1-A2): Basics, daily interactions, school support
  • Intermediate (B1-B2): Grammar fluency, independent composition
  • Advanced (C1-C2): Study abroad & corporate-level proficiency

3. School Tuition (K-12)

Comprehensive academic support focusing on conceptual clarity and top board results.

  • Boards: CBSE, ICSE, and Tamil Nadu State Board
  • Subjects: Maths, Science, English, Social, Hindi, French, Tamil
  • Integrated foundation for younger learners

4. Spoken Courses & Voice Culture

  • Spoken Hindi for Professionals: Sector-specific terminology (IT, Finance, Medical) and conversational practice.
  • Vani Vikas (Elocution & Voice Culture): Phonetics, pronunciation, speech drafting, and stage presence.
  • Spoken English: Grammar, public speaking, group discussions, and interview skills.
