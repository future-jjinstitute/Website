General Information

The JJ Institute of Hindi Studies is a premier global destination for linguistic research and Hindi language education, established in 2009 to promote academic excellence and cultural understanding.
Our head office is in Kotagiri, Nilgiris, with branches in Peelamedu and Thudiyalur, Coimbatore. We also offer robust digital learning platforms for global accessibility.
Our vision is to bridge cultural gaps and empower individuals with professional Hindi proficiency, fostering a global community of scholars dedicated to the language.
Yes, our curriculum is aligned with national standards and we facilitate examinations recognized by major Hindi prachar sabhas and educational boards.
Yes, we welcome prospective students for campus tours. Please schedule your visit at least 48 hours in advance through our admin office.

We offer courses from Absolute Beginner (Aksharabhyas) to Advanced Professional levels, including Parichaya, Prathamik, and specialized diploma tracks.

Durations vary; basic levels typically last 3-4 months, while advanced diplomas can span 6-12 months.

Admissions & Enrollment

Applications can be submitted via our online student portal or in person at any of our branches.
Eligibility varies. Beginner levels have no prerequisites; higher levels require previous certificates or a placement test.
Fees are structured per level. Please request a detailed brochure via email or check the course section on our website.
Yes, merit-based and need-based scholarships are available for deserving students. Applications open twice a year.
Enrollments usually close 3 days before the batch start date. Check the news marquee for the latest batch updates.
Refunds are subject to our withdrawal policy: 90% refund before batch starts, 50% within the first week.
Yes, we have many international students who join via our online portal. Special orientation is provided for non-native speakers.
We accept UPI, Net Banking, Credit/Debit cards, and Bank transfers. Cash is accepted at branch offices.
Yes, students can take a "semester break" and rejoin the next available batch within 6 months.
Only a proficiency placement test is required for those not starting at the Absolute Beginner level.
