Founding in Tamil Nadu

Founded in 2009, JJ Institute of Hindi Studies emerged as a response to a growing need: the desire of non-Hindi speakers in Tamil Nadu to master the national language for professional and personal growth.

Starting as a small learning center, we recognized early on that language is more than just communication—it's a bridge to culture, opportunity, and unity. Our methodology was specifically tailored to overcome the unique linguistic challenges faced by native Tamil speakers.
